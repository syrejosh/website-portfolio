Understanding the Difference

User Experience (UX): Focuses on the overall feel and usability of a product. It's about understanding user needs, creating intuitive flows, and ensuring the product is easy and enjoyable to use.
User Interface (UI): Focuses on the visual design and aesthetics of a product. It's about creating beautiful, consistent, and visually appealing interfaces that users interact with.
